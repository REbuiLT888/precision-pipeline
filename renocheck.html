Common questions

What people ask before they upload.

What types of quotes or files can I upload?
+

Almost anything you have — a builder's quote as a PDF, renovation quote, shopping list from Bunnings or Reece, architectural plans, renovation drawings, photos of your space, or screenshots from a supplier website. We work with whatever you've got. Supported file types include PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX, XLSX, and DWG/DXF plan files.

How long does it take to get a report?
+

The free quick check is returned within 24 hours of upload on business days. A paid Full Quote Review ($99+) takes up to 48 hours. A full Material Comparison ($249+) or Plan Takeoff ($499+) takes up to 5 business days depending on project complexity.

Are you a building company? Can you manage my renovation?
+

No. We are a sourcing and procurement service — we do not hold builder's licences, manage construction, or provide building advice. If you need licensed trades for plumbing, electrical, gas, waterproofing, or structural work, you will need to engage them directly or through our trade introduction service. We can introduce you to suitable independent local installers where appropriate, as a disclosed referral arrangement.

Are the savings guaranteed?
+

No, and we'll never claim they are. Potential savings depend on the specific products, your location, current freight costs, lead-time requirements, and installation requirements. What we give you is an accurate comparison of alternative supply options so you can make an informed decision — not a headline claim. Our comparisons are based on current supplier pricing at the time of the review.

What happens to my quote information?
+

Your quote information is handled in accordance with Australian Privacy Principles. We do not share your specific quote details with third parties (including suppliers we compare) without your consent. We use aggregated, anonymised quote data to improve our pricing intelligence — but your personal project details are confidential.

Do I have to buy materials through you?
+

No. You receive the comparison report and can use it however you like — take it back to your builder, source materials yourself, or ask us to source them. There's no obligation to purchase through RenoCheck. If you do want us to source, we charge a procurement fee (5–12% of supplied materials depending on order size) and will disclose this clearly upfront.

What are material packs? How are they different?
+

Material packs are pre-curated collections of products for a specific room type — bathroom, kitchen, laundry, or pre-sale refresh. Each pack comes in three tiers: Fast Local (Australian-stocked, 3–7 day delivery), Factory Direct (better margin, 4–6 week lead), and Premium (higher-specification finish). Packs are designed to make the supply decision simple when you already know the room scope.

Do you work with builders and trades, or just homeowners?
+

Both. Homeowners use us to understand and compare their builder's quote. Builders and trades use us as a wholesale supply channel — particularly for factory-direct products where we can improve their own margin. Property managers and real estate agents use our pre-sale refresh packs to prepare properties for sale or rental. If you're a trade interested in a B2B account, contact us through the form above.
