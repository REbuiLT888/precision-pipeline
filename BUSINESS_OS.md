## Outreach Flow

**Step 1 — First SMS:**
> Hey mate, quick one — noticed [Business Name] has strong reviews but either no website or not much of a proper online setup. I help local trades turn their Google reviews into a proper website / quote system. Want me to send over a quick free audit?

**Step 2 — If reply yes:**
> No worries. I'll take a quick look and send back some ideas. Can you fill this out quickly so I know what you're actually trying to improve — [Survey Link]. Once I've got that I'll send back a simple recommendation and mockup.

---

## Survey Questions (keep short — tradies won't fill long forms)
1. Business name
2. Trade/service type
3. Do you have a website?
4. What do you want most?
5. Where do most jobs come from now?
6. Average job value?
7. Could you handle more enquiries?
8. Do you have a business email?
9. Open to monthly marketing if the plan makes sense?
10. Best contact number

1. Add 50–100 new leads
2. Score them
3. SMS best 30–50
4. Track replies
5. Send audit survey to interested leads
6. Generate audit
7. Create mockup
8. Send proposal
9. Call within 24 hours
10. Close deposit
11. Move to fulfilment
